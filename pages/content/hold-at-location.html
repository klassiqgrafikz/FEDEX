Hold for Pickup

Have your packages held for pickup at a nearby FedEx location.

Overview

FedEx Hold at Location lets you redirect packages to a convenient FedEx location for secure pickup. Perfect for when you cannot be home to receive a delivery.

Secure Pickup

Packages are held securely at the location until you arrive. Government-issued ID required for pickup.

Extended Hold

Packages can be held for up to 7 business days, giving you plenty of time to retrieve them.

No Charge

Hold at Location is a free service included with your FedEx shipment at no additional cost.
