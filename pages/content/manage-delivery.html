Manage Your Delivery

Customize your delivery preferences and manage shipments.

Overview

FedEx Delivery Manager gives you control over your incoming packages. Choose delivery options that fit your schedule and preferences, all from one convenient dashboard.

Delivery Options

Redirect to a nearby FedEx location, schedule a specific delivery time, or request weekend delivery.

Hold at Location

Redirect packages to a FedEx retail location for secure pickup at your convenience.

Notifications

Get customized alerts about your package status via email, text message, or the FedEx mobile app.
