Overview

FedEx Corp. is composed of multiple operating companies that work together to provide comprehensive logistics, transportation, and business solutions worldwide.

FedEx Express

The worlds largest express transportation company, providing time-definite delivery to over 220 countries.

FedEx Ground

North American ground delivery network providing cost-effective, day-definite service for packages.

FedEx Freight

Less-than-truckload freight services with fast transit times and comprehensive coverage across North America.
